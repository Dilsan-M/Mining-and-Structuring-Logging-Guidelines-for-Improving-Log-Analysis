1. Broad High-Recall Query (Recommended Starting Point)

This will retrieve repositories containing explicit logging guidance documents.
2. Query Focused on “What to Log”
("what to log" OR "log levels" OR "when to log" OR "structured logging" OR "sensitive information")
("logging" OR "application logs")
(path:/docs/ OR filename:README.md)
Useful for extracting actionable recommendations such as:
what events should be logged,
log levels,
sensitive data handling,
tracing,
observability.
3. Engineering Handbook / Internal Standards Query
Many mature projects store logging guidance inside engineering standards rather than standalone logging docs.
(logging observability tracing telemetry)
("engineering standards" OR handbook OR conventions OR practices OR guidelines)
4. Query for Structured Logging Practices
("structured logging" OR "correlation id" OR "trace id")
("best practices" OR guidelines OR standards)
This is especially useful for distributed systems and microservices research.
5. Query for Security/Sensitive Logging Guidance
(logging OR logs)
("sensitive data" OR PII OR GDPR OR credentials)
("guidelines" OR "best practices")
This often reveals high-quality operational logging rules.
6. Query for Log Levels and Severity Policies
("log level" OR ERROR OR WARN OR INFO OR DEBUG)
(logging guidelines OR logging conventions)
This helps extract recommendations about:
severity assignment,
verbosity,
debugging practices,
production logging.

("logging guidelines" OR "logging best practices")

("logging guidelines")

(logging OR observability)
extension:md
(guidelines OR practices OR standards)

observability,
telemetry,
diagnostics,
tracing.

(observability OR telemetry OR diagnostics OR tracing)
(logging guidelines OR standards OR practices)
